# 🔍 Be an AI Detective!

**Is it real or made by AI? Check the clues!**

| | Clue | Ask yourself |
|---|---|---|
| 👀 | **Look closely** | Extra fingers? Things melting or disappearing? |
| 🤔 | **Does it make sense?** | Can a cat really dive like that? |
| 👂 | **Listen** | Does the voice sound like a robot? Do the lips match the words? |
| 📍 | **Who posted it?** | Is it someone I know and trust? |
| 🙋 | **Ask an adult** | Not sure? Check with a grown-up! |

## 🛑 The Detective Rule

If something online asks for a **secret**, **money**, or a **password**, or says "don't tell your parents":

### STOP ✋ THINK 🤔 CHECK 🔍 ASK 🙋

💛 Detectives use their powers for good. Never make fakes of friends, even as a joke.

😠 Someone being mean online or in a game? Don't reply. Save it. Tell a teacher or parent. Telling is not tattling!

🧠 AI is not for doing your homework. Your brain is the boss!
