| ✅ | Mission | How |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | 🔑 **Make a family secret password** | Pick a silly word only your family knows, like "purple pancake." Keep it secret! |
| ☐ | 🎬 **Play Real or AI?** | Show your family a video and ask: "Real or AI? What is your clue?" |
| ☐ | ✋ **Teach the pledge** | Stop, Think, Check, Ask, with the hand moves! |

## 📞 For grown-ups

AI can copy voices from short recordings. If you get a call that sounds like a loved one asking for money or help, hang up and call back on a number you already know, or ask for your family password.
