# Teacher Guide: 30-Minute Session

**Audience:** grades 3 to 5, one class at a time
**Materials:** laptop with videos saved offline, speaker, projector adapter, small prizes
**Golden rule:** about 8 to 10 slides. Leave time to play and talk.

| Time | Activity | What kids do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 min | Hello and Player Select | Raise hands: who plays games, speaks two languages, has talked to AI |
| 3 to 7 min | You Be the AI | Shout "cat!" three times, then guess the mystery animal |
| 7 to 15 min | Real or AI? | Stand up if REAL, hands on head if AI, then name a clue |
| 15 to 19 min | Which voice is real? | Show 1 or 2 fingers for Clip A or Clip B |
| 19 to 22 min | Kind Detective | Talk: never make fakes of friends, tell an adult |
| 22 to 28 min | Prize quiz | Show 1, 2, or 3 fingers, winners get prizes |
| 28 to 30 min | Family Mission and pledge | Stop, Think, Check, Ask with hand moves |

## Game 1: You Be the AI

Show three cat pictures and say "cat!" each time. Then show a dog and ask, "What is it?" Some kids will shout "cat!" Say: "AI only knows what it was shown. It can be wrong. Your brain is the boss!"

## Game 2: Real or AI?

Play a funny AI animal video and a real amazing animal video. Kids stand up for REAL and put hands on their heads for AI. Ask: "What clue did you see?"

**Tip:** save every video on your laptop and test it with Wi-Fi off. School networks often block video sites.

## Game 3: Which voice is real?

Record yourself saying one sentence. Make the same sentence with a free text-to-speech tool. Kids guess which is real. Then say: "AI can copy voices. If a call sounds like Mom but asks for something strange, hang up and call back the number you know."

## Prize quiz tips

- Everyone answers with fingers first, so every child plays.
- Pick a winner from the correct answers.
- Out of prizes? Winners become Detective Captains and lead the pledge.
- Test every question on an adult first. If it confuses a grown-up, it will confuse kids.

## Words to use and avoid

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Critical thinking | Think like a detective |
| Deepfake (alone) | A fake picture, video, or voice made by a computer |
| Personal information | Your secrets: name, address, school, passwords |
| Cyberbullying | Someone being mean online or in a game |

## Key messages to protect

- **AI is never for doing your homework.** Your brain is the boss.
- **Never make fakes of others,** even as a joke.
- **Telling is not tattling.** If something online feels wrong, tell a trusted adult.
